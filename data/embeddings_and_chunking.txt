An embedding is a list of numbers that represents the meaning of a piece of text. Texts with similar meaning get vectors that point in similar directions, so a computer can measure how related two texts are by comparing their vectors. The most common measure is cosine similarity, which looks at the angle between two vectors rather than their length.

Before documents can be searched, they are split into chunks. Chunks that are too large blur several topics together, while chunks that are too small lose context. A common approach is to use chunks of a few hundred words with a small overlap between neighbouring chunks, so a sentence near a boundary still appears with its surrounding context. Each chunk is embedded once and stored, and at question time only the question needs to be embedded.
